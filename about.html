About Mercel

I built MAi-RAG-PA over 6 months — a privacy-first local AI assistant with RAG knowledge bases, memory, roles, and self-healing agentic workflows. It runs entirely on local hardware via Ollama, with vector DB storage in Qdrant, memory in SQLite, and no cloud dependency.

Like I said, that's not a side project. That's a product. And it's open source — because I believe your data should stay yours, not end up in some corporate cloud you're paying for monthly.

I help small businesses do the same: install and configure private AI systems on their own machines, handle document ingestion, build custom roles. Fixed scope, fixed price, no open-ended contracts.

I don't do open-ended projects. That's not just a service choice — it's a professional boundary that protects you from scope creep and me from burnout.
