About Mercel

Professional websites, digital services, and open-source tools for small businesses that want to grow online — without the complexity or ongoing fees.

Our Mission

At Mercel, we believe small businesses deserve the same professional online presence as large corporations — without the enterprise price tag or the endless monthly fees.

Our mission is simple: Help small businesses look professional online, reach more customers, and save time with practical digital services.

Who We Are

Mercel is a freelance digital services provider specializing in professional websites, local SEO, branding, automation, and open-source AI tools for small businesses.

We understand that every business is different. A restaurant needs different things than a law firm or an e-commerce store. That's why we don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions — we build what you actually need, not what we want to sell.

We're also the creators of MAi-RAG-PA, an open-source AI framework that runs entirely on your hardware. Every client gets free access to this tool — no cloud dependencies, no monthly fees, no data privacy concerns.
